## Reject for this portable alpha

- The proposed nested runtime tree of contracts, schemas, playbooks, examples,
  and source subdirectories. A flat reference layer is easier for Codex to
  retrieve, package, and validate.
- Wholesale import of all 56 review sources. The runtime ledger includes only
  sources that support an actual rule and whose identity was locally checked.
- Persistent case memory or third-party personality profiles.
- Cultural, personality, status, manipulability, or deception scoring.
- Jurisdiction-specific legal or HR modules without qualified ownership and a
  current-source maintenance process.
- Branded practitioner frameworks as evidence authorities.
