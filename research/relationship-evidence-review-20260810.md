## Scoring consequences

The evidence supports structured reflection but not a single scientifically
validated compatibility number. The product therefore uses an explicitly
custom **Decision Fit Score**:

- the user chooses the decision and dimensions;
- each dimension has observable anchors and an evidence note;
- confidence and evidence coverage are reported separately;
- user priorities can change weights without pretending that weights are
  universal;
- safety stops and deal-breakers remain outside the average;
- scores are versioned snapshots and may change after correction or new facts;
- typology and low-specificity cues cannot create numerical precision.

## Knowledge gaps retained

- The custom Decision Fit Score has no predictive validation and must not be
  marketed as a psychological test or compatibility instrument.
- Most relationship studies rely partly on self-report and group averages.
- The evidence does not establish thresholds for continuing, escalating,
  reconciling, or ending a specific relationship.
- Legal definitions, emergency resources, and institutional duties remain
  current and jurisdiction-specific.
- Fresh behavioral qualification is still required for the new score and
  dossier workflows.
