## Evidence rules for implementation

1. A meta-analysis can support a general association or heterogeneity claim;
   it cannot prove a specific person's motive.
2. A primary experiment can motivate a branch or contraindication; it cannot
   become a universal script.
3. A practitioner framework must be labeled as a scaffold, not “the science.”
4. Official guidance must be checked for jurisdiction and date before
   substantive use.
5. Chinese–English adaptation must be based on role, channel, audience, and
   observed local norms. Do not turn national-level averages into personal
   assumptions.
6. Every runtime claim should link to a source ID, include a limitation, and
   have a review date.
