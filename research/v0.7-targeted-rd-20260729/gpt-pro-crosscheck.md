## Useful Pro findings

- The current skill is a bounded decision-support kernel, not yet a deep
  strategist.
- The highest-value gaps are mechanism diagnosis, evidence governance,
  conflict/repair, power/voice/fair process, feedback, negotiation, boundaries,
  stakeholder mapping, relationship maintenance, and bilingual pragmatics.
- A compact eight-module reference architecture is preferable to expanding the
  kernel.
- Famous methods such as *Getting to Yes* should be labeled as practitioner
  frameworks rather than presented as a unified validated intervention.
- Feedback can backfire; task focus, actionability, commitment, and review
  matter.
- Scene playbooks should contain exposure-calibrated options, wording,
  response branches, observation windows, and triggers.
- MBTI, hidden influence scoring, and manipulation tactics should remain out.

## Source-quality defect found during verification

The architecture was useful, but the sample source ledger was unreliable.
Seven of ten supplied DOI-to-claim mappings were demonstrably wrong:

| Pro mapping | Verification result | Corrected source |
|---|---|---|
| `10.1111/peps.12173` for psychological safety | DOI is an unrelated book review | `10.1111/peps.12183` |
| `10.1037/0033-2909.125.4.422` for feedback | Does not identify the cited Kluger–DeNisi review | `10.1037/0033-2909.119.2.254` |
| `10.5465/amr.2001.4614948` for justice | Does not identify the Colquitt meta-analysis | `10.1037/0021-9010.86.3.425` |

| `10.1016/j.jom.2013.08.001` for negotiation | DOI is *Bracing for demand shocks* in operations management | use De Dreu et al. (2000), `10.1037/0022-3514.78.5.889`, plus practitioner-scaffold caveats |

Therefore:

- apply the architecture only after local verification;
- do not copy the Pro ledger into runtime references;
- use `source-ledger.md` in this folder as the corrected starting point;
- require claim-level source checks as a v0.7 acceptance gate.

## Classification

### Apply

- mechanism-driven architecture;
- eight total method modules;
- scene playbooks;
- evidence tiers and claim governance;
- contraindications and counterexamples;
- stronger behavior evals and bilingual mirrors.

### Consider

- textual stakeholder interface maps;
- role-play coaching;
- optional user-facing source notes.

### Reject

- all unverified Pro citations;
- personality typing and hidden scoring;
- donor-specific content;
- a claim that more feedback, more disclosure, or more conflict is inherently
  beneficial.

### Needs user decision

- workplace-first versus equal everyday-domain depth;
- functional bilingual parity versus deeper native-language content review;
- internal versus normally visible citations.
