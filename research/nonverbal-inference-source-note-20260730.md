## Stable identity

- Lisa Feldman Barrett, Ralph Adolphs, Stacy Marsella, Aleix M. Martinez, and

  Human Facial Movements*

- `PMCID PMC6640856`
- `DOI 10.1177/1529100619832930`
- *Psychological Science in the Public Interest*, 20(1), 1-68

Authoritative identities checked through PubMed and the publisher DOI record on
2026-07-30.

## Candidate permitted claim

Facial movements can carry socially relevant information, but configurations of
facial movement vary across people, situations, and cultures and are not a
specific, context-free readout of one emotion category.

## Candidate runtime rule

Describe facial movement as an observation. Do not infer a person's emotion,
honesty, consent, diagnosis, motive, engagement, or future behavior from facial
movement alone. Combine context, explicit words, records, follow-through, and a
decision-relevant clarification.

## Limitations

- The review focuses on facial movement and common emotion categories; it does
  not establish that every nonverbal cue is meaningless.
- It does not validate a complete workplace-coaching protocol.
- It does not justify replacing one body-language dictionary with another model
  of hidden internal state.
- Additional evidence would be needed for claims about voice, posture, gaze,
  disability, neurodivergence, or multimodal AI systems beyond the bounded rule
  above.
