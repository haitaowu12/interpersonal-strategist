# Method Contracts

Use this reference when a case is high-stakes, compound, recurring, or likely
to produce an overconfident interpretation. The contracts are internal quality
controls, not forms to paste into the answer.

## Shared reasoning envelope

Route the case before advising:

- **IN_SCOPE:** ordinary interpersonal decision support.
- **COACH_WITH_CAUTION:** advice is useful, but power, privacy, uncertainty, or
  formal-process proximity requires narrower claims and lower exposure.
- **REFER_OR_ESCALATE:** immediate safety, crisis, or a substantive legal, HR,
  medical, safeguarding, or investigative determination belongs with a
  qualified current route; help the user prepare facts and questions.
- **REFUSE:** coercion, deception, retaliation, humiliation, surveillance,
  sabotage, privacy invasion, or pressure after refusal.

Label material inputs as:

- **record:** copied or directly inspectable wording;
- **observation:** what the user directly perceived;
- **report:** what another person said occurred;
- **interpretation:** meaning assigned to an event;
- **unknown:** missing or contradictory information.

Use these confidence bands:

- **OBSERVED:** directly present in a record or observation;
- **SUPPORTED:** multiple relevant items favor the claim and material
  counterevidence is limited;
- **PLAUSIBLE:** consistent with the evidence but alternatives remain live;
- **SPECULATIVE:** possible but weakly supported;
- **UNKNOWN:** the available evidence does not discriminate.

Confidence and urgency are separate. Low confidence plus high urgency calls
for protection, documentation, reduced exposure, and clarification—not an
accusation and not inaction.

For a consequential recommendation, generate two to four hypotheses. For each,
track supporting evidence, counterevidence, a predicted observation, an
observation that would weaken it, confidence, and decision relevance.

Compare candidate actions with an ordinal 0/1/2 check:

1. goal fit;
2. information gain;
3. reversibility;
4. protection;
5. exposure cost;
6. escalation risk;
7. user capability gain.

Do not add the scores into fake precision. Use them to expose trade-offs and
select the smallest move that can protect the user or improve the decision.

Every complete action needs a purpose, primary move, channel, example wording,
positive/ambiguous/negative/no-response branches, observation window,
escalation trigger, and stop condition.

## A. Situation reading

**Inputs:** chronological evidence, actors, user goal, deadline, prior actions.

**Produce:** facts, interpretations, unknowns, two to four hypotheses,
discriminating observations, the minimum decision-changing unknown, and
whether immediate action is needed.

**Fail if:** chronology is invented, silence becomes proof, or one explanation
is presented as certainty.

## B. Signal classification

Classify the observable signal, not the person's character:

- content;
- process;
- timing;
- audience or status;
- resource or decision;
- relational;
- threat or formal risk;
- noise or insufficient evidence.

Record whether it is explicit, indirect, or inferred; isolated, repeated, or
pattern-unknown; how reliable and decision-relevant it is; alternatives; and
the next best observation. Response latency alone cannot establish intent.

## C. Power and dependency

Map formal decision rights, informal influence, controlled resources, mutual
dependencies, alternatives, audiences, retaliation or exposure paths,
protective allies or routes, reversibility, and recommended exposure.

Fail if title is treated as total power or voice is recommended without
examining who bears the downside.

## D. Conflict diagnosis

Classify task, process, relationship, values, identity or face, rights or
authority, and safety issues. Note whether the conflict is latent, explicit,
escalating, stalemated, de-escalating, or in repair; whether it is mutual,
asymmetric, or possibly coercive; the escalation loop; audience effect;
de-escalation preconditions; and the smallest process intervention.

Do not label possible abuse as mutual conflict or assume task conflict is
beneficial.

## E. Negotiation planning

Map the user's interests, hypotheses about the counterpart's interests,
constraints, credible alternatives, non-negotiables, tradable issues,
objective criteria, two or three feasible packages, questions that reduce
uncertainty, and a pause or walk-away condition.

Compile any agreement into owner, deliverable, date, dependencies, decision
authority, review point, warning point, and fallback.

## F. Feedback and accountability

Identify whether the purpose is learning, correction, recognition,
documentation, or consequence. Specify observable behavior, context, standard,
impact, requested change, perspective question, support, agreed action,
verification date, and consequence if repeated.

Fail if a personality judgment substitutes for behavior or accountability has
no owner and review point.

## G. Trust repair

Classify the affected dimension as ability, benevolence, integrity, mixed, or
unknown. Separate verified breach, disputed interpretation, and responsibility
status. Check acknowledgment, supported responsibility, impact recognition,
remedy, prevention, restitution, a bounded re-entry trial, verification
behavior, review window, and stop condition.

An apology is not proof of change. Forgiveness cannot be demanded, and full
trust should not be restored before proportionate verification.

## H. Boundary and escalation

Specify the behavior or request, user's limit, allowed alternative, what the
user will do next, wording, repetition rule, needed record, support or
authority route, safety check, escalation threshold, and disengagement
condition.

A boundary governs the user's action. It does not claim control over another
person. Translation must preserve the refusal and consequence.

## I. After-action review

Record the prior prediction and confidence before the outcome, action taken,
actual response, evidence changed, hypotheses strengthened or weakened,
decision quality, outcome quality, reusable rule, next observation window, and
whether to continue, monitor, escalate, or close.

Do not rewrite the prediction after the result or judge the decision only by
whether the outcome happened to be favorable.

## Shared prohibitions

No method may output a diagnosis, legal or HR finding, unsupported motive,
deception verdict from style or demeanor, coercive action, guaranteed outcome,
or action without a stopping condition.
